# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: artisans and creatives in French-speaking (and Pidgin-speaking) Africa — tailors, hairdressers, jewelers, electronics repairers, caterers — deciding whether to download African Puzzle WORKS. Many have low literacy; the app itself is audio-first ("plus besoin de lire ni d'écrire"). Secondary: NGOs, partners, and funders assessing the project's credibility.

## Product Purpose

Marketing website for the African Puzzle WORKS mobile app. It presents what the app does, shows the real product in action (device mockups of actual screens), and converts visitors into app users while giving partners confidence the project is real and well-built. Success: visitors watch the intro video, understand the app in under a minute, and download or get in touch.

## Positioning

The whole craft business in your pocket: clients, measurements, programmes, appointments, portfolio, and payments — one app made for artisans.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: watch the intro video; contact/sign-up as the conversion action.
- Secondary CTA: explore features / partner contact.
- The line a visitor remembers: an app that runs your craft business even if you don't read or write.
- Belief ladder: (1) this app is made for artisans like me, in my language; (2) it handles my real workflow — contacts, measurements, programmes, rendez-vous, photos; (3) I don't need to read or write to use it (audio guidance); (4) it's affordable and honest — pay once, nothing renews; (5) it's real and trustworthy (real screens, real team, real countries).
- Proof on hand: real app screenshots (simulator captures), intro video, team page, countries-of-reach section.

## Brand Personality

Warm, capable, proud. Celebrates craft and craftspeople; confident and human, with joyful color. Speaks plainly (FR first), never condescends about literacy — audio-first is framed as strength, not deficit.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero metric grids, gradient-text headlines, endless identical feature cards).
- Charity/aid visual language that frames artisans as beneficiaries instead of professionals.
- Cold corporate fintech aesthetics.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real product — actual app screens in device mockups beat illustrations.
2. FR is the source voice; every word must survive translation to Pidgin.
3. Audio-first is a feature, not a footnote — the "no reading or writing needed" story leads.
4. Warmth through the brand's own colors (purple #3D2664, gold #FFAA00) and photography of craft, not through default cream/pastel washes.
5. Mobile-first: most visitors arrive on the same phone the app will run on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum. Reduced-motion alternatives for all animations. Low-literacy audience: short sentences, strong visual storytelling, video/audio pathways. Three site locales: fr (default), en, pcm.
